Receiver, quarterback or play-caller?

A ridge regression on 300,000 targets (2009–2026) credits every target's EPA to the receiver, the passer, the play-caller and the defense, after adjusting for down, distance, field position, score and quarter. Each breakout season's EPA per target above league average splits into those parts and sums exactly. "Receiver" = his career estimate plus whatever that season beat the model by (form and luck).

Context, season by season

QB lift = his QB's EPA per target when throwing to him minus to everyone else in the same games (a breakout raises this mechanically). Vacated = share of last season's team targets by players no longer on the team.

Season by season

How this was built

Data
nflverse play-by-play, weekly stats, participation (who was on the field, 2016–2025), Next Gen Stats separation, and FTN charting (reads, catchable, contested, drops; 2022+). Regular season only. Fantasy points are nflverse PPR. 2026 runs through Week 5.
Routes
Routes = team dropbacks with him on the field (participation), so YPRR and TPRR exist for 2016–2025. 2026 participation is not published yet, so 2026 uses snap share instead.
Shares
Team totals are counted only over games he played, for the team he played for in each game.
2006–2008
Old play-by-play only names the receiver on completions, so target-based numbers start in 2009. Fantasy ranks for those years still count.
Credit model
Ridge regression with receiver, passer, play-caller and defense-season terms plus situation controls; penalty chosen by cross-validation over games; 80% intervals from 30 game-level resamples. Play-callers come from a hand-checked table (sources below); unlisted team-seasons use the head coach.
Signals
Features come only from the season before (no look-ahead). Leave-one-season-out testing: each season is predicted by a model that never saw it. With 13 breakouts in the test window, every interval is wide.
How it was checked
Four analysis squads each had an independent checker recompute three published numbers by a different route, plus reviewers looking for definition errors, look-ahead and overclaiming. Yards and targets match nflverse weekly totals; play-by-play yards can differ from official totals by a few yards.
